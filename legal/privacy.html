Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how FRIDAY ("we", "the app") collects, uses, and protects information in connection with its SMS-based date-planning service.

What we collect

  • Phone number — used to send and receive SMS messages related to the service.
  • Calendar availability data — free/busy time ranges from Google Calendar and/or Apple Calendar (via CalDAV), used only to find mutually available times for proposed dates.
  • Preference and feedback data — information derived from your responses (accept, reject, rerun, ratings) used to improve future suggestions.
  • Message content — the text of messages you send to the service, used to process your requests.
  • GitHub repo data – any repo updates or CI/CD failures from GitHub actions

How we use this information

Information collected is used solely to operate the service: proposing mutually available times, suggesting activities based on stated preferences, and processing replies. We do not use this information for advertising.

Information sharing

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Calendar and message data is not sold, rented, or shared with third parties, except:

  • With service providers strictly necessary to operate the app (e.g. Twilio for SMS delivery, Google/Apple for calendar access, GitHub, our AI model provider for generating suggestions), each bound by their own data-processing terms.
  • Where required by law.

Data retention

We retain data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us using the information below.

Opting out

Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages. Reply HELP for assistance. Standard message and data rates may apply.

Security

We take reasonable technical measures to protect your information, including encrypted storage of calendar access credentials.
